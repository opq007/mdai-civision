Add an account

In your browser: sign in at modelscope.ai, then DevTools → Application → Cookies → https://www.modelscope.ai → select all rows and copy them as one name=value; name=value string. m_session_id and h_uid are required — they carry the signed-in state.
The service keeps sending this browser's User-Agent. A session created by Firefox but used with a Chrome User-Agent is an identity change upstream risk control can see. For Camoufox, choose Firefox; paste its exact UA in the custom field if it differs from the preset.
Automatic proxy assignment is off by default. Keeping the import IP avoids changing egress right after the session was created.
